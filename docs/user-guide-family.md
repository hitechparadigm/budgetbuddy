# Family Collaboration User Guide

**Last Updated**: 2026-02-01

## Overview

BudgetBuddy allows you to share your budget with a family member or partner. This guide explains how to invite someone to your family account, accept invitations, and manage family members.

## Getting Started

### Who Can Use Family Features?

- **Primary User**: The account owner who can invite and manage family members
- **Spouse/Partner**: Full access to view and edit budgets and transactions
- **Viewer**: Read-only access to view budgets and transactions

### Family Limits

- Maximum 2 members per family (you + 1 partner)
- Invitations expire after 7 days
- Only one pending invitation per email address

## How to Invite a Family Member

### Step 1: Open Family Settings

**Web App**:

1. Click on **Settings** in the navigation menu
2. Scroll down to **Family Settings** section

**Mobile App**:

1. Tap the **Settings** tab
2. Tap **Family Settings**

### Step 2: Send an Invitation

1. Enter your partner's email address
2. Select a role:
   - **Spouse**: Can view and edit budgets and transactions
   - **Viewer**: Can only view budgets and transactions (read-only)
3. Tap/click **Send Invitation**

### Step 3: Wait for Acceptance

- Your partner will receive an email with an invitation link
- The invitation is valid for 7 days
- You can see pending invitations in Family Settings

## How to Accept an Invitation

### If You Already Have an Account

1. Check your email for the invitation
2. Click the **Accept Invitation** link
3. Log in to your BudgetBuddy account
4. You'll be added to the family automatically

### If You're New to BudgetBuddy

1. Check your email for the invitation
2. Click the **Accept Invitation** link
3. Create a new account with your email
4. Complete the registration process
5. You'll be added to the family automatically

## Managing Family Members

### Viewing Family Members

**Web App**:

- Go to **Settings** → **Family Settings**
- See all family members with their roles and join dates

**Mobile App**:

- Go to **Settings** → **Family Settings**
- Pull down to refresh the member list

### Changing a Member's Role

_Primary user only_

1. Go to **Family Settings**
2. Find the member you want to update
3. Tap/click the role dropdown
4. Select the new role (Spouse or Viewer)
5. Confirm the change

### Removing a Family Member

_Primary user only_

1. Go to **Family Settings**
2. Find the member you want to remove
3. Tap/click **Remove** or the trash icon
4. Confirm the removal

**Note**: Removed members will be moved to their own family account.

## Leaving a Family

_Spouse and Viewer only - Primary cannot leave_

1. Go to **Family Settings**
2. Tap/click **Leave Family**
3. Confirm your decision

**What happens when you leave**:

- A new family account is created for you
- You lose access to the shared budgets
- Your transaction history is preserved
- You can start fresh with your own budget

## Roles and Permissions

### Primary User

- ✅ Send invitations
- ✅ Change member roles
- ✅ Remove members
- ✅ View all budgets and transactions
- ✅ Create and edit budgets
- ✅ Create and edit transactions
- ❌ Cannot leave family (must transfer ownership first)

### Spouse

- ❌ Cannot send invitations
- ❌ Cannot change member roles
- ❌ Cannot remove members
- ✅ View all budgets and transactions
- ✅ Create and edit budgets
- ✅ Create and edit transactions
- ✅ Can leave family

### Viewer

- ❌ Cannot send invitations
- ❌ Cannot change member roles
- ❌ Cannot remove members
- ✅ View all budgets and transactions
- ❌ Cannot create or edit budgets
- ❌ Cannot create or edit transactions
- ✅ Can leave family

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I have more than 2 people in my family?

No, BudgetBuddy currently supports a maximum of 2 members per family (you and one partner). This is designed for couples managing household finances together.

### What happens to my data if I leave a family?

When you leave a family:

- A new family account is created for you
- You start with a fresh budget
- Your personal transaction history is preserved
- You lose access to the shared family budgets

### Can I transfer primary ownership?

Currently, primary ownership cannot be transferred. The original account creator remains the primary user.

### What if my invitation expires?

If an invitation expires after 7 days:

- The primary user can send a new invitation
- The old invitation link will no longer work
- There's no limit to how many times you can resend

### Can I see who made changes to the budget?

Yes, all changes are tracked with the user who made them. You can see:

- Who created a transaction
- Who last updated a budget
- When changes were made

### Is my data secure when sharing with family?

Yes, your data is secure:

- All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- Family members can only access their own family's data
- Role-based permissions prevent unauthorized changes
- All actions are logged for audit purposes

## Troubleshooting

### Invitation Not Received

1. Check your spam/junk folder
2. Verify the email address is correct
3. Ask the primary user to resend the invitation
4. Contact support if the issue persists

### Cannot Accept Invitation

- Make sure the invitation hasn't expired (7 days)
- Ensure you're logged in with the correct email
- Check if the family is already full (2 members)

### Cannot Remove Member

- Only the primary user can remove members
- You cannot remove yourself (use "Leave Family" instead)
- Make sure you're logged in as the primary user
